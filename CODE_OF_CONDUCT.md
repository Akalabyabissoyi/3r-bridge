# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers and people from every discipline and career stage, and
keep discussion about animal research civil and evidence-based. Harassment is not tolerated.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at bissoyi.akalabya@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially, and
the maintainer may warn, edit or remove contributions, or ban participants, where needed.
